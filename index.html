field_ops/

The evening run

One command against the day's export. It returns the cases to act on tomorrow and the per-enumerator summary for the morning meeting. A duplicated household ID found on day three is a phone call; found at analysis it is a hole in the sample nobody can fill, because the team has left and the respondent cannot be re-identified.

Enumerator monitoring

Look, do not conclude

An enumerator whose households report much lower consumption may be fabricating, or may have been sent to the poorest block. No statistic separates those, and the difference is somebody's job. Comparisons are leave-one-out and can be held within village, which is the only version that is interpretable.

Back checks

The one thing that does separate them

A supervisor re-asks a short subset of the questions. Classify them first: sex of the household head should never differ between two competent interviews, last month's income legitimately may. One error rate pooled across both makes an honest enumerator asking hard recall questions look worse than a careless one.

DHS worked example

Checked against the figures DHS published

survey_tools/dhs_stunting.R

Child stunting by wealth quintile

Estimates stunting from a DHS children's recode with design-based intervals, then compares the result against the figures DHS published: 35.5 percent nationally for India's NFHS-5, 46.1 in the poorest wealth quintile falling to 22.9 in the richest. Reproducing that table is the only cheap proof a survey pipeline is right end to end.

InsightStack

Where the data comes in

The loader that turns a raw DHS recode into the CSV this script consumes. The two are coupled through a file rather than a dependency, so neither repository needs the other installed.

Open the loader →

Two DHS specifics are handled here and both return a wrong number rather than an error: subgroups go through svyby on the whole design rather than a filtered data frame, and design effects use deff = "replace", because v005 is a relative weight and the default returns values like 165 where the answer is 1.11.
